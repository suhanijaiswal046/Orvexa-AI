# HR Policy

## Paid Time Off
Employees receive 18 days of paid time off per calendar year. Unused days may be carried forward up to 5 days, subject to manager approval.

## Work Hours
The standard workday is 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday. Flexible arrangements require manager approval.

## Remote Work
Employees may request up to 2 remote workdays per week. Approval depends on role requirements and manager agreement.
